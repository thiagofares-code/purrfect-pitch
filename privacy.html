Privacy policy

Purrfect Pitch collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your scores and progress are saved only on your own headset (browser local storage) and never leave it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them.

The game reads your headset and controller positions to let you play. This happens on the device and nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
